# Laurel Portie: The 7-Dollar Facebook Strategy

This document outlines the strategic implementation of Laurel Portie's $7/day Facebook advertising framework for the MPDC ecosystem. The goal is to build a high-trust audience of men in crisis and convert them into the $27 "Mastery System" with minimal ad spend.

## 🎯 The Core Philosophy
Instead of running expensive "Cold to Sold" ads, we run **Video Awareness** ads at a very low budget ($1/day) to identify who is actually interested in the content. We then only spend the "heavy" part of our budget ($4/day) on people who have already demonstrated high intent (watched 50%+ of our content).

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: The "Power Content" (3 Videos)
We need 3 distinct videos running simultaneously at **$1/day each**.

| Video Type | Topic Focus | Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Video 1: Authority** | "Why most divorce advice for men fails." | Establish Greg as the expert voice. |
| **Video 2: Emotion** | "The day I realized my life wasn't over." | Build relatability and hope. |
| **Video 3: Tactical** | "One simple mindset flip for immediate relief." | Provide instant value/quick win. |

### Tactical Setup:
- **Campaign Type**: Video Views (Awareness)
- **Daily Budget**: $1.00 per video.
- **Targeting**: Abroad (e.g., Men 30-55, interests in Divorce, Fatherhood, Self-Improvement).
- **Placement**: Mobile Feeds & Reels exclusively.

---

## 🔄 Phase 2: The Retargeting Engine ($4/day)
This is the "Salesman" campaign that only talks to the "Warm" audience created in Phase 1.

### The Audience:
- Any visitor who watched **50% or more** of any of the 3 Power Content videos.
- This creates a "Filter" – we don't waste money showing the offer to people who scrolled past the video in 2 seconds.

### The Ad:
- **Campaign Type**: Sales (Conversions)
- **Offer**: Join the MPDC Mastery System for $27 (50% Off Promo).
- **Creative**: A direct-to-camera "Invite" mentioning the specific pain points discussed in the content videos.
- **Daily Budget**: $4.00.

---

## 📈 Projected Unit Economics
- **Total Ad Spend**: $210 / Month ($7/day)
- **Target CPV (50% View)**: $0.10 - $0.30
- **Daily Warm Lead Pool**: 20–40 highly engaged men.
- **Break-Even Point**: 8 sales per month ($216 revenue).
- **Goal**: 15+ sales per month (2.0x+ ROAS on the Frontend).

## 🧬 Why this works for MPDC
Men in post-divorce trauma are skeptical. They don't buy from strangers. The $1/day videos allow them to "get to know" Greg and his philosophy before being asked to pay. By the time they see the $27 offer, it feels like a natural next step from a trusted mentor.
