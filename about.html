About Us

Our Story

I’m Alex Morgan, a divorce recovery coach who helps men navigate one of the most challenging transitions of their lives with clarity, structure, and purpose.

Like many men, my own divorce required me to re examine my identity, priorities, and direction. It was a difficult period that demanded honest self reflection and intentional rebuilding. Through that process, I learned that simply moving on is not enough. Lasting progress comes from understanding what changed, what was lost, and how to rebuild from a stronger foundation.

That experience led me to focus on helping other men do the same. My work centers on guiding men through practical recovery by regaining confidence, re establishing personal standards, and creating stability after divorce.

This work is not about revisiting the past or assigning blame. It is about growth, responsibility, and building a future that feels solid, intentional, and aligned with who you want to become.
