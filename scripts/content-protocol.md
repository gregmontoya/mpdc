# MPDC AI Content Generation Protocol
To ensure high-performance SEO ranking and bypass AI detection flags, all content must follow these structural and tonal rules:

## 1. Embedded Randomness (Sectional Tone Dithering)
- **Section Tones**: Do not use a consistent tone throughout the entire piece. 
  - *Intro*: High-stakes, punchy, conversational.
  - *Technical Sections*: Analytical, logic-first, "cold" data-driven.
  - *Closing*: Inspiring "expert peer" persona.
- **Micro-Tone Shifts**: Use short, staccato sentences followed by one longer, rhythmic sentence to avoid "AI cadence."

## 2. Format Rotation (Pattern Interrupts)
Predictability is the enemy of engagement and AI bypass. 
- **Rotation Pattern**: Paragraph -> Bullets -> Paragraph -> Specific Callout (Blockquote) -> Data Table or FAQ -> Paragraph.
- **Visual Entropy**: Use HTML-style callouts (`<div id="quick-answer">`) to break the flow of standard markdown.

## 3. Tone Modulation (The Jargon-Translation Bridge)
- **The "Expert-Technical" Switch**: Introduce a high-level technical term (e.g., *Neurological Grounding*, *Hedonic Adaptation Error*) and then immediately explain it in "blue-collar" terms.
- **Linguistic Markers**: Use "MPDC-specific" jargon to reinforce brand authority (e.g., *Reality Chain*, *Frame*, *Biological Pause*).

## 4. Controlled Entropy (Intentional Structural Imperfection)
- **Sentence Variation**: Intentionally use a "sentence fragment" for impact.
- **Structure Break**: Don't follow a rigid 3-point list every time. If an article traditionally has 4 sections, move the conclusion or the "Next Step" to somewhere slightly unexpected.
- **Human-Like Interjections**: Use interjections like "Here’s the thing," or "Wait—look at the data again."

## 5. Structural Consistency (The MPDC Standard)
Despite the entropy, every post must include:
- **Title**: High-intent, keyword-rich.
- **Quick Answer**: A summary box for Google's featured snippets.
- **TOC**: Anchor-linked table of contents.
- **Internal Linking**: Minimum 3 links to other MPDC resources.
- **AEO Summary**: A JSON-LD schema-ready summary in frontmatter.
