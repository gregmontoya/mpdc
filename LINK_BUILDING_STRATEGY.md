# 🔗 MPDC Link Building & Authority Blueprint
*The Off-Page Strategy for Dominating the Men's Divorce Recovery Niche*

## 🔝 The "Authority Gap" Strategy
Link building in 2026 isn't about volume; it's about **relevance** and **trust**. Since MPDC deals with "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) content, we need links from high-authority, established entities in the Legal, Health, and Lifestyle sectors.

## 🏛️ Tier 1: Digital PR & Media (The "Authority" Links)
**Goal:** Get mentioned in high-DA (Domain Authority) publications to boost site-wide trust.

*   **Platform:** [Connectively](https://connectively.us/) (formerly HARO) & [Featured.com](https://featured.com).
*   **The Angle:** Position Greg as an expert on "The Biological Stress of Divorce" and "Identity Reconstruction."
*   **Target Queries:** 
    *   "Men's Mental Health in 2026"
    *   "Financial recovery after legal battles"
    *   "Co-parenting with high-conflict exes"
*   **Backlink Value:** DA 70-90+ (e.g., Newsweek, Men's Health, Forbes).

## 🤝 Tier 2: The "Legal Network" (The "Context" Links)
**Goal:** Get links from websites that your target audience is already visiting during the divorce process.

*   **Targets:** Family Law Attorneys, Mediation Services, and Accountants.
*   **The Strategy:** The "Resource Page" Pitch.
*   **The Hook:** "We noticed you have a resources page for clients. We've built a 'Divorce Panic Protocol' (free asset) specifically for men that helps stabilize them so they become better clients for you."
*   **Backlink Value:** DA 20-50 (Hyper-relevant to the niche).

## 🎙️ Tier 3: Podcast & Interview Circuit
**Goal:** Humanize the brand and gain "Show Note" backlinks.

*   **Targets:** *The Art of Manliness*, *Order of Man*, *The Divorce Survival Guide*.
*   **The Strategy:** Guest appearances.
*   **The Deliverable:** Every guest appearance includes a backlink to `/join` or a specific blog post in the show notes.

## 💎 Step 4: Building "Link Magnets" (Earned Links)
Instead of asking for links, we create things people *want* to link to.

1.  **MPDC Divorce Timeline Calculator:** A tool that estimates recovery time based on psychological inputs.
2.  **State-by-State Divorce Rights Guide:** A curated resource of legal "Quick Facts" for men.
3.  **The "Divorce Recovery Index":** An original study (survey) on the mental health of men post-divorce.
